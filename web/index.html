What is being computed

SNR² = T × [Ṡ²σo³/(16π√π Ḃ)] × ε ηN(z) × r. The first two factors describe the patient and the acquisition time and are the same for every system compared, so the plots show the system part, ε ηN r σo³, in units of T Ṡ²/(16π√πḂ). ηN is the axial sensitivity profile of the optimised multi-bed acquisition; r is the resolution factor, in its non-TOF form for systems without time of flight, which is why those depend on the object diameter. Curves are the minimum over the scan range.
